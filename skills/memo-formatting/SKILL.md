# Skill: Business Memo Formatting

## Overview
This skill guides the AI to format unstructured analytical, financial, or strategic notes into a professional, executive-ready business memorandum.

## Objectives
1. Structure raw input into a standard formal memo header (TO, FROM, DATE, SUBJECT).
2. Organize key takeaways into concise executive summaries and bulleted body sections.
3. Apply professional business formatting and ensure action-oriented recommendations.

## Inputs Required
- **Raw Content/Notes:** Draft thoughts, bullet points, data findings, or analysis.
- **Target Recipient & Sender:** Names and titles (optional; defaults to placeholders if omitted).

## Processing Rules & Guidelines
- **Structure:**
  - Header block at top (`MEMORANDUM`, `TO`, `FROM`, `DATE`, `SUBJECT`).
  - **Executive Summary:** First section; 2–3 sentences highlighting the core message and recommendation.
  - **Context & Analysis:** Clear headings, bold key terms, and bulleted breakdowns.
  - **Next Steps / Recommendations:** Action items with clear ownership or sequence.
- **Tone & Style:**
  - Concise, direct, and authoritative (direct-style business communication).
  - Use sentence case for subject lines and section titles.
  - No decorative prose or meta-commentary ("Here is your memo").
